# How to Convert JPG to PDF Online Without Losing Quality

Converting images into PDF documents is useful for receipts, scanned pages, forms, portfolios, and document submissions. The best workflow depends on whether you need one image or a multi-page document.

## Choose the right page layout

A photo may be landscape while a document is portrait. If the converter stretches the image to fill the page, text can become distorted.

A better workflow preserves the original aspect ratio and fits the image inside the selected page dimensions.

Use [Image to PDF](/tools/image-to-pdf) when you need to turn one or more images into a PDF directly in the browser.

## Multiple images

For scanned documents, each image should become a separate PDF page. Sort the files before conversion so the final document follows the correct order.

If you already have several PDF files, use the [PDF Merger](/tools/pdf-merger) instead of converting them again.

## Quality and file size

Higher JPEG quality usually creates larger PDFs. For text-heavy scans, very aggressive compression can make small characters difficult to read.

If your PDF is too large after conversion, use [PDF Compressor](/tools/pdf-compressor) as a separate step.

## Privacy

Receipts, IDs, contracts, and forms can contain sensitive information. Whenever possible, choose a browser-based workflow where files are processed locally.

## A simple workflow

1. Prepare and rename your images.
2. Open [Image to PDF](/tools/image-to-pdf).
3. Add the images in the desired order.
4. Check page orientation and sizing.
5. Generate the PDF.
6. Open the result and verify every page.

## FAQ

### Can I convert JPG to PDF on a phone?

Yes. A browser-based converter works on modern mobile browsers.

### Will JPG quality improve when converted to PDF?

No. A PDF container does not magically increase image resolution. Start with the highest-quality source available.

### Can I merge several JPG files into one PDF?

Yes. Convert the images to pages and export one multi-page PDF.
