# ROI Calculator Guide: How to Calculate Return on Investment

Return on investment, or ROI, is a simple way to express gain or loss relative to the amount invested.

## The basic formula

`ROI = (Return − Investment) / Investment × 100`

If you invest $1,000 and receive $1,250, the gain is $250 and ROI is 25%.

Use the [ROI Calculator](/tools/roi-calculator) for a quick calculation.

## ROI is not the whole story

Two investments can have the same ROI but very different durations. A 25% return over one month is not equivalent to 25% over ten years.

For meaningful comparisons, consider:

- time period
- risk
- cash flow timing
- fees and taxes
- liquidity

## Marketing ROI

For advertising, revenue alone can be misleading. Include relevant campaign costs when defining the investment amount.

For example, if a campaign generates $5,000 in revenue but costs $2,000 in ad spend and $500 in production, the result depends on which costs your business definition includes.

## Negative ROI

If the return is lower than the investment, ROI is negative. That is not a calculation error; it indicates a loss relative to the starting amount.

## FAQ

### Can ROI exceed 100%?

Yes. A gain equal to the original investment is 100% ROI; larger gains produce percentages above 100%.

### Is ROI the same as profit margin?

No. ROI uses an investment denominator, while margin generally uses revenue or selling price.

### Should taxes be included?

For a real decision, use a consistent definition that includes the costs relevant to the comparison.
