# TTS in Education: Making Learning More Accessible

Text-to-Speech supports differentiated learning by offering audio versions of course materials, enabling multitasking, and supporting students with reading difficulties.

## Use Cases in Education

- Audio versions of lecture notes and assignments.
- Listening practice for language learners.
- Assistive reading for students with dyslexia.

## Implementation Suggestions

- Offer separate speed and pitch controls for learners.
- Provide short audio segments to aid memory retention.
- Integrate with note-taking tools to combine audio and text.

## Actionable Takeaways

- Provide teacher- and student-facing controls; keep options simple.
