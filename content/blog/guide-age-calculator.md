# How to Calculate Age From a Date of Birth Accurately

Age looks like a simple subtraction problem, but an accurate calculation needs to account for whether the birthday has already occurred this year.

## The basic method

Start with the current year minus the birth year. Then check whether the current date is before the birthday.

If the birthday has not happened yet, subtract one.

For example, someone born on December 10, 2000 is not yet 26 on August 22, 2026. Their completed age is 25.

## Why dates are tricky

Dates can be represented in local time, UTC, or different calendar formats. A robust application should parse dates consistently and avoid ambiguous strings such as `01/02/2026`, which can mean different things in different regions.

ISO format, `YYYY-MM-DD`, is generally easier to exchange between systems.

## Completed age vs approximate age

Most everyday age calculators return completed years. Legal, financial, or medical contexts may use additional rules, so a general calculator should not be treated as a legal determination.

## FAQ

### Does a leap-day birthday cause problems?

A February 29 birthday needs a clear rule for non-leap years. Different jurisdictions and applications may handle the date differently.

### Can I calculate age on a phone?

Yes. A browser-based calculator works without installing an app.

### Can the calculator show months and days?

A basic year calculation is straightforward; a detailed calendar calculation needs careful month-length handling.
