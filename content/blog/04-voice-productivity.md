# Voice Productivity: Use STT to Work Faster

Voice-first workflows reduce friction for note-taking, drafting, and ideation. This article shows how to design apps that tap into human speech for faster output.

## Workflows That Benefit

- Fast drafting of emails and documents.
- Meeting summarization and action-item extraction.
- Hands-free note-taking for fieldwork.

## Design Tips

- Offer templates for common content types (emails, blog drafts).
- Auto-detect language and suggest voice settings.
- Let users apply simple commands like "new paragraph" or "comma" if supported.

## Tooling

- Use local STT for drafts; optionally apply server-side NLP for summarization.
- Expose quick-export features: clipboard, TXT, and cloud sync.

## Actionable Takeaways

- Build voice-first micro UX for repetitive tasks.
- Keep manual editing simple after transcription.
